Localization and RTL

Hebrew and Arabic are right-to-left; English and Russian are left-to-right. The conversation language can differ from the app language. This board holds the direction rules, formatting rules and the core copy deck with medical strings flagged. Filter the deck by area or show only strings that need clinical review.

Direction rules

  • Set dir on the root from the conversation or app language (I18nManager.forceRTL + reload on native; <html dir> on web). Use start / end, never left / right, in styles.
  • Mirror: navigation order, back and chevrons, bubbles (user at end), progress bars, sheets' close button, list rows, swipe actions.
  • Do not mirror: glucose graphs and their time axis, time-in-range bars, clocks, media and voice controls, the orb, brand logo, numbers.
  • Charts keep time left to right in every locale; RTL screens show “Time runs left to right” under the chart.
  • Numbers, units and times are isolated LTR runs (unicode-bidi: isolate / \u2066…\u2069) so “124 mg/dL” never reorders.
  • Use Western digits 0–9 for glucose, carbs and doses in all locales by default. Arabic-Indic digits are an open decision.
  • Units (mg/dL, mmol/L, g, U) are never translated.
13.2

Formatting, grammar and review

Formatting and grammar

  • Messages in ICU MessageFormat (FormatJS / i18next-icu). Plurals: en 2, ru 4, he 3, ar 6 forms.
  • Hebrew: second person written in gender-neutral forms (infinitive, impersonal, plural). Companion persona sets its own grammatical gender via select.
  • Arabic: Modern Standard Arabic, second person masculine as default with a feminine variant set on the account (open decision); no letter-spacing, no all-caps, line height +12%.
  • Russian: formal вы; allow 30% longer strings; buttons wrap to two lines before truncating.
  • Dates and times: Intl.DateTimeFormat with locale; 24-hour for he, ar, ru by default; week starts Sunday for he and ar, Monday for ru.
  • Decimal separator follows locale for display (5,6 mmol/L in ru) but parsing accepts both comma and dot.

Review workflow

  • Every non-English string ships with the Translation review label until a native speaker signs off.
  • Strings marked Clinical review need sign-off from a clinician in each market before release, in every language.
  • String ids are stable; screenshots per locale are generated from Storybook / Expo previews for reviewers.
  • The phones on this canvas use these strings; Onboarding and Companion are complete in all four languages.
