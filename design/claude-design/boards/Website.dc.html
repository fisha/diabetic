How it works

Talk, log, and look back, without the busywork

01

Tell it, or snap it

Say “124 after lunch”, type it, or photograph your plate. You check every number and unit before anything is saved.

02

It follows up

Short check-ins at times you choose, with quiet hours. If you already logged it, the question is withdrawn.

03

Patterns, with receipts

Observations always show the records behind them, and say plainly when they’re only a first impression.

The first few days are about learning your routine. Understanding how your body responds takes much longer, and Steady says so.
