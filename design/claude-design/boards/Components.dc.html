12.4

Provenance badges

SuggestedAcknowledgedYou reportedDevice recordedEstimateYou confirmedWeighedUnsureEditedPending syncNot synced

Provenance badges are text first, with an optional 14 pt icon. These are four separate facts and are never merged: a suggestion being acknowledged does not mean the action was reported, and a reported dose is not a device record.
