14.3

Gaps

Known gaps, stated plainly

  • Meal, Overview, Journal, Routine, Settings, Membership and the web dashboard are prototyped in English only. Their strings are listed in the copy deck pattern but not yet translated; RTL behaviour for them follows the rules on board 13 and is specified, not demonstrated.
  • The web dashboard bars have a click readout but no hover / scrub tooltip on a continuous graph.
  • Website is fluid but the canvas shows the desktop width; the 375 px layout is specified in Handoff 15.5, not drawn.
  • Tablet layouts for the app are specified (two-pane at 768 px and up), not drawn.
  • Guardian accounts, care-team sharing and device connections are designed as Planned states only.
  • Low-glucose guidance, insight wording and the medical disclaimer are placeholders pending clinical and regulatory review.
