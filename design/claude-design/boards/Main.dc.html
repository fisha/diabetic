Steady Working name

A calm AI companion for people living with type 1 diabetes

Complete interactive design reference for the Expo / React Native app (iOS, Android) and the Next.js website and dashboard. The phones on this canvas are rendered with web technology to demonstrate behaviour faithfully. They are not production React Native code; every component is mapped to its intended native implementation in the handoff boards.

Labels used throughout

Demo data: simulated values, not a device Planned: designed, not active Clinical review: copy needs sign-off Translation review: medical term

Navigation model

CompanionVoice home, text, history
OverviewGraph, summaries, insights
JournalTimeline, edits, history
ProfileTreatment, devices, settings
Quick log opens as a bottom sheet from the conversation and from Overview and Journal headers. It never stacks a second modal; a sheet replaces its own content step by step.
Context continuity. Voice, text, cards and dashboard share one conversation id. Leaving and returning lands on the same thread and scroll position.
Notifications deep-link into the exact conversation turn or reminder (steady://companion/c/[id]?turn=).
Membership lives in Profile. It never appears inside a health conversation.
